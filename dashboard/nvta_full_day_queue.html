WHY THE QUEUE NEEDS A STATE

A pointwise read of speed cannot be a queue

vehicles · adjacent-bin change

Reading Q straight off each bin's speed gives swings of vehicles between adjacent bins, because 1/v amplifies speed noise at low speed and the formula never uses Q(t−1). Driving the recurrence with a smooth arrival profile brings the largest step down to vehicles without smoothing anything.

CROSS-METHOD GATE

The QVDF prior has no resolution on this link

peak queue vs assumed capacity

The QVDF arrival rate is an S3 inversion of speed, which returns served flow and is capped at capacity by construction. Arrivals and discharge therefore become two scalings of the same assumed capacity, and the queue follows the assumption rather than the data.
